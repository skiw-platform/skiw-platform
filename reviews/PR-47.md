### [P1] Recover when the switch command itself fails

- **Rule:** spec H3 steps 6–7, D15; deployment failure handling.
- **Evidence:** `infra/host/skiw_host/deploy.py:207–208` replaces the state and then runs `compose up`. At `:245`, `switch()` is outside the recovery block beginning at `:247`. Its exception goes straight to `main()`.
