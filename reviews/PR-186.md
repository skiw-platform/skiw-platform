### Findings

#### [P1] The refused rollback leaves retained audit records unprotected

- **Category:** defect.
- **Rule:** TEN-05, TEN-06, TEN-07, M4-rest Decisions 32 and 33.
- **Evidence:** The new guard is in `app/accounts/migrations/0022_security_event_agency.py:26`. By then `0025_security_event_shape.py:100` has dropped the event shape trigger and `0024_security_event_rls.py:37` has dropped all three policies and disabled and unforced RLS. The `skiw_app` SELECT and INSERT grants from `0002_auth_throttle_security_event.py:82` still apply. Django reverses migrations individually, and DECISIONS line 1852 explicitly records that those earlier reversals persist. The existing prefix-walk assertion starts after the protections are gone and wraps the whole walk in an outer rollback (`test_write_grants_live.py:155`, `:175`, `:212`).
- **Failure scenario:** On an isolated PostgreSQL database at 0030, I ran the real management command to reverse to accounts 0021 with 32 seeded change events present. It exited 1 with the intended refusal and preserved all 32 rows. Before the command, RLS, FORCE and the shape trigger were present. After the command, all three were absent, the applied accounts head was 0022, and the app role retained SELECT and INSERT. I verified the result through PostgreSQL catalogs and a row count.
- **Impact:** A runtime DB connection can read retained change events without tenant binding and insert change-kind records without the trigger's shape and provenance checks. This is a persisted database state after failure. The normal previous-image rollback does not reverse migrations.
- **Required correction:** Refuse a schema rollback with retained change events before it can commit removal of either the shape trigger or the RLS policies. Keep 0030's grant revocation independently reversible. Keep forward migrations compatible with databases already upgraded. Update the rationale in DECISIONS.
- **Owner:** builder.
- **Verification:** Run a committed reverse from the current head against a disposable database containing real seeded change events. Assert the refusal, retained rows, active shape trigger, ENABLE + FORCE RLS and the expected policies. Verify the runtime role cannot see change events without tenant binding or insert change-kind records directly. Verify the intended no-event downgrade still works.
- **Status:** open.
