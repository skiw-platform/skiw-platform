### What I checked
- **Order and faults in `counted_assertion`.**
  - The challenge is taken first. Then `counted` reserves. Its precheck `_discover` runs in autocommit, and its check `_verify` runs inside `with_agency()`. `then(row)` runs in the same transaction.
  - A fault in `_discover` comes before the comparison, so the attempt is released.
  - A fault in `record` (`then`) is inside the check before it returns, so the attempt is released and the passkey use rolls back (tested).
  - A wrong assertion keeps the count and feeds the lock. That matches TOTP step-up (`verify` also uses `feeds_lock=True`).
- **`record` for a passkey.** It still requires the session user's own factor of a listed kind, in use. The `_verify` conditional update has just proved the row is in use in the same transaction, so `record` cannot fail on a race. `recent()` stays kind-agnostic, and nothing else reads `FACTOR_KINDS`.
- **Begin.** It runs in the wrapper's transaction and writes only the session's challenge record, which is saved at the response, as the registration begin is. A user with no passkey in use gets 409 and no challenge (tested). Begin counts nothing; it is signed-in only and about the user themselves.
- **The builder's equivalent mutant (the `_verify` user and agency comparison): I agree.** `counted._check` loads the active user by the same `user_id` and agency that `_discover` compared, so through the one entry the two can't differ. Keeping it as defence in depth is fine.
- **The builder's question 2 ([A] evidence outlives removal): the question doesn't arise in practice.** My probe below shows that removing the passkey through the route cancels the step-up at once. So nothing needs to be built, and only the docs are wrong (P3 #1).

### Findings

#### P3 #1 — the runbook and DECISIONS say removing a passkey leaves its step-up valid; through the route, it doesn't
- **Evidence:**
  - Runbook "Step up with a passkey": "Removing a passkey does not cancel a step-up it proved a moment earlier in that session: it lapses after `SKIW_STEPUP_MAX_AGE_MIN` (5 minutes) or when that session ends."
  - DECISIONS M3g-1d-2 says the same in its "[A] Evidence outlives removal".
- **My probe** (scratch copy, review DB):
  1. Step up with a passkey; the step-up probe gives 200.
  2. `POST /api/auth/factors/passkey/remove` for that passkey gives 200 `removed`.
  3. The probe now gives **403**, while the evidence row is still stored.
- **Why:** `factor_change._changed` calls `login.reissue`, which cycles the session key. `recent()` reads evidence by the current key's hash, so the old evidence no longer applies to this session. Other sessions are ended by the marker.
- **Where the [A] still holds:** a retirement that doesn't go through the route, such as the test's direct update, or a future owner reset. The existing test (`test_a_retired_passkey_cannot_step_up_but_its_earlier_evidence_still_counts`) covers only that.
- **Impact:** none for security. The real behaviour is stricter than documented, but an operator reading the runbook would expect the wrong thing.
- **Correction:** reword the runbook line and the [A]. For example: "removing it through the factor routes ends every step-up of every session, because the session is re-issued; evidence naming a passkey retired any other way keeps counting for its age." Optionally pin it with one route-level test.

#### P3 #2 — test gap: when over the budget or locked, nothing shows the challenge is spent
- **Mutant R1** moved `take_challenge` inside the precheck, so it is taken only after a successful reservation. It **survived** all 436 tests across the step-up, sign-in, step-up evidence, step-up route and CSRF suites.
- The module docstring and DECISIONS say the challenge is taken first, "so it is spent whatever follows". Nothing checks that for the `limited` answer, at sign-in or at step-up.
- **Low stakes:** a challenge left behind is still single-use (the conditional update) and expires at its own deadline.
- **Correction:** add `assert passkey.CHALLENGE not in signin.session_of(client)` to `test_past_the_shared_budget_nothing_is_looked_up` and to the locked case of `test_wrong_passkeys_and_codes_at_step_up_share_the_lock_with_sign_in`. Add the same to their sign-in twins.
