### [P2] The runbooks never let production through staging's SSH allowlist
- **Category:** runbook gap (UNI-16; `roles/reviewer.md` §7 "executable by an operator").
- **Rule:** UNI-16; G7 SSH policy.
- **Evidence:** `host-rebuild.md:13` tells the operator to put "the operator's and the off-site
  host's CIDRs" in `SKIW_SSH_ALLOW`; the new `staging_status_key` section (`host-rebuild.md`
  "Making `staging_status_key`") and `deploy.md` "Before the first deploy" never say that staging
  must also allow production's address. `nftables.conf` drops new SSH from any other source under
  `allowlist`.
- **Failure scenario:** staging uses `allowlist` (G7). Every production deploy fails closed with

  working production deploy.
- **Required correction:** one step in `host-rebuild.md` (staging, with the status key) and the
  `deploy.md` production prerequisites: under `allowlist`, add production's outbound address to
  staging's `SKIW_SSH_ALLOW` and re-run `apply`; under `fail2ban`, note the ban risk (or add it to
  `ignoreip` if the jail supports that here).
- **Owner:** Builder.
- **Verification:** re-read the two runbook sections.
- **Disposition:** before merge.
- **Status:** open.
